Coffee for everyone

EZ Coffee is a local café committed to creating an inclusive and welcoming space, both in person and online. Our website is designed to be usable with screen readers, keyboards, and high‑contrast settings.

What makes us so accessible

  • Clear navigation: Simple consistent menu on every page.
  • Readable text: High contrast and good sizes.
  • Keyboard friendly: All interactive elements are reachable by keyboard.
  • Descriptive labels: Forms and buttons clearly describe their purpose.
